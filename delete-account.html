Delete your Sukoon account

This page is for the Sukoon app by the Sukoon team. You can delete your account at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sukoon and go to Settings.
  2. Under Your account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything.

Your account is deleted straight away, together with everything Sukoon keeps on that phone.

Without the app

Email lifesigns.comms@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject Delete my Sukoon account. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • On our server: your email address, your account id, and your daily smart-chat usage counts. Nothing else about you is kept there.
  • On the phone, when you delete in the app: your family members, check-ins, prescriptions and their photos, and any backups or reports waiting to be shared.

Backup files you saved or shared elsewhere are yours and are not touched; delete them where you keep them. Requests already handled by the AI services are covered by their own terms (see the privacy policy); Sukoon's server never stored them.
